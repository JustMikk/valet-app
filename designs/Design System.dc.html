VALET WORKFORCE · DESIGN SYSTEM v0.1

Calm by default.
Loud when it matters.

Derived from the reference: monochrome surfaces, one oversized number per screen, floating pill chrome, and color reserved for meaning. Built mobile-first for staff outdoors and admins on the move. Every token below exists in light and dark; the accent is tenant-swappable (Regency gold by default).

01 — PRINCIPLES

1One big numberEach screen leads with the single fact that matters: start time, time on shift, people needing you.
2Color = meaningSurfaces stay black and white. Green, orange, red and blue only ever describe status — always paired with an icon and words.
3Gold = actThe tenant accent marks the one primary action per view. Never decoration, never status.
4Floating chromeTop bar and tab bar are elevated pills; content scrolls beneath. Actions live in the thumb zone.
5Honest timeRelative time first ("late 12 min"), venue-local clock second. Location is a punch snapshot, never "live".

06 — RULES

DoUse inline var(--token) values only · keep one accent action per view · pair every status color with icon + words · show "venue time · PT" where ambiguous · keep primary staff actions in the bottom 40% of the screen.
Don'tUse gold for status · show moving dots or "last seen" · hide unavailable actions (disable + explain instead) · show bill rates on any staff surface · rely on hover for anything.
